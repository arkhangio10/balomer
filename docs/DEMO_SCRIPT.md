# Demo script

Target: a three minute video. Nothing is narrated that is not on screen.

> Draft. Rewrite once the walking skeleton runs end to end.

## 0:00 — The problem (20s)

An amateur coach has one phone on a tripod and no analyst. After the match the
footage sits unwatched. Decisions at halftime are made from memory.

## 0:20 — The setup (20s)

One fixed camera above the pitch. One laptop at the side. A Fire TV in the
clubhouse. Nothing else. Show the three, physically.

## 0:40 — Live radar and stats (50s)

Fire TV screen: players as dots on a 2D pitch, teams separated by colour, ball
tracked. Cut between the real pitch and the radar so the mapping is obvious.
Show possession and territory updating live.

## 1:30 — A minute alert (25s)

Land on a 15' or 30' alert as it fires. Read what it says. Point at the metric
on screen that produced it.

## 1:55 — The halftime report (45s)

Three recommendations. For each one, show the number it is grounded in. Say
plainly: the agent writes the prose, the edge worker computes the numbers, and
the agent is not allowed to invent one.

## 2:40 — Privacy and the close (20s)

Video never left the laptop. Only coordinates and metrics went to AWS. Name the
stack in one sentence and end on the full-time report.

## Shot list

- [ ] Camera on its mount, wide
- [ ] Laptop terminal with the edge worker running
- [ ] Fire TV: radar, live
- [ ] Fire TV: a minute alert firing
- [ ] Fire TV: halftime report, all three recommendations legible
- [ ] Fire TV: full-time report
- [ ] Split screen, real pitch next to radar
