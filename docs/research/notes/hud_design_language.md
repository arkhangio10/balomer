# "FIFA 2027": game identity and in-match HUD / broadcast design language (as of 21 Oct 2026)

Scope note: the game a user calls "FIFA 2027" is EA SPORTS FC 27. EA lost the FIFA name in 2023, so there is no "FIFA 27" from EA. Material on FC 24, 25 and 26 and on FIFA 15/23 is labelled as **prior edition**. Fetched sources say little about how the in-match HUD looks (positions, colours, motion). Most of the concrete, sourced detail comes from EA's own accessibility and settings documentation and from the 2023 brand identity write-ups. Anything else is marked as a gap or an inference.

## 2. How is the in-match HUD laid out (score bug, clock, radar/mini-map, player indicators, stamina, momentum/pressure) and what changed versus FC 26 / FC 25?

### Takeaway
EA's own settings and accessibility docs say the in-match HUD is built from a small set of toggleable modules:
- a **Score Clock** (scoreboard plus timer, with a team crest) and an optional **Score Clock Dropdown** that hangs extra info beneath it;
- a **Radar** (2D, 3D or Off) with user-selectable team colours;
- a **player indicator** over the controlled player, sized Small/Default/Large, that **fades as stamina drops**, plus an optional **player name bar** and overhead info (name, PlayStyles+ icons, next-switch indicator);
- contextual overlays (Tactical Suggestions notifications, scrolling line-ups, input overlay, connection monitor).

The documented FC 27-specific HUD changes are small: **custom per-teammate player-indicator colours** and **opaque "High Contrast Menus"**. No official source documents a redesigned score bug or a momentum meter for FC 27.

  - No separate HUD-scale or HUD text-size option for matches is described.
  - Source: [EA Accessibility – FC 27](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/ea-sports-fc/fc-27)
- **FC 27 player indicators** — [EA Accessibility – FC 27](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/ea-sports-fc/fc-27):

### Inferences
- The default score bug is a compact, always-on module with a collapsible "dropdown" tier beneath it. The dropdown carries secondary context: plausibly scorers, cards or added time, but the docs only say "extra info". That two-tier pattern (persistent compact bar plus a transient strip below it) maps directly onto live-stats overlays.
- Stamina is shown by **fading the controlled player's indicator**, not by a separate bar (no stamina-bar toggle exists in the FC 26/27 settings lists). This is a low-clutter way to encode a continuous variable on an existing marker.
- FC's closest feature to a "momentum/pressure indicator" is the **Tactical Suggestions Notification** and **Tactical Chalkboard** overlays. No documented momentum bar exists. The exact visuals of the Chalkboard were not found.
- Because scoreboards are a separate, swappable asset class, community "scoreboard packs" re-skin them per competition. Mods for FC 26 replicate LaLiga, Copa del Rey, FA Cup and DFB-Pokal bugs ([FIFA Infinity – scoreboard packs, community mods](https://dl.fifa-infinity.com/fc-26/kos-mega-scoreboard-pack-26/); [mck Ko-fi – Spain pack, community mod](https://ko-fi.com/s/2465bcc695)). This suggests the game uses per-competition scoreboard variants, but the official list was not verified.

## 3. What visual design language does the presentation use (palette, dark vs light, gradients, typography, glass/flat, iconography) and how do overlays animate?

### Takeaway
The only documented design system behind FC's look is the 2023 **EA SPORTS FC brand identity**:
- Designed by **Uncommon Creative Studio**, with motion by **Buck** and custom type by **F37 Foundry**.
- An **inverted-triangle** marque taken from the in-game player-control marker, set on an **isometric triangular grid**.
- A **boot black / chalk white** base with **pitch green / forest green** accents.
- Two bespoke typefaces: a Cruyff-inspired sans with triangular ink traps, and a Marta-inspired serif.

In-game, FC 27's menus use **translucent tiles** by default; the new High Contrast Menus option swaps them for opaque ones. No source describes in-match overlay animation curves or timing.

### Cited Findings
- Credits: Uncommon (London) led the identity, **Buck** did motion design and 2D animation, and **F37 Foundry** produced the type. — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/uncommon-creates-triangle-based-identity-for-ea-sports-fc/); [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/uncommon-creative-studio-ea-sports-fc-graphic-design-130423); [F37 Foundry – EAFC custom fonts](https://f37foundry.com/custom-fonts/eafc) (page not opened); [Design Week](https://www.designweek.co.uk/issues/10-april-14-april-2023/ea-sports-fc-identity-uncommon/) (page not opened)
- The logo is an **inverted triangle** drawn from "the player control indicator above each athlete in matches", the triangular polygons of game geometry, and passing triangles and set plays. Haider Muhdi (design director, Uncommon): "Hiding in plain sight for almost 30 years, the inverted triangle became our playmaker." — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/uncommon-creates-triangle-based-identity-for-ea-sports-fc/)
- Nils Leonard (co-founder, Uncommon): "Without knowing it, the new logo had been in our hands for almost 30 years." The FC initials sit in a triangular grid, and the mark has three versions: primary logo, two-letter product prefix, and **authenticity mark**. — [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/uncommon-creative-studio-ea-sports-fc-graphic-design-130423)
- The triangle is embedded in an **isometric grid system** that underpins the whole design system. — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/uncommon-creates-triangle-based-identity-for-ea-sports-fc/)
- **Palette:** main hues **Boot Black** and **Chalk White**, supporting hues **Pitch Green** and **Forest Green**. The palette is meant to "act as a canvas for the multicoloured nature of tribal football fandom". No hex values were published in the sources read. — [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/uncommon-creates-triangle-based-identity-for-ea-sports-fc/)
- **Typography:** two custom families.
  - A sans whose stems are shaped by the **Cruyff turn**, with ink traps that follow the triangular grid.
  - A serif inspired by **Marta**, for editorial and "elevated brand moments".
  - Both are multilingual, covering Latin and Arabic scripts.
  - Source: [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/uncommon-creates-triangle-based-identity-for-ea-sports-fc/); [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/uncommon-creative-studio-ea-sports-fc-graphic-design-130423)
- **Motion:** Buck's motion system is "an integral piece of the puzzle". Its aim was to give the identity "the energy of the game", meaning structure, dynamics and forward momentum. The moving assets build on the triangle. — [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/news/uncommon-creative-studio-ea-sports-fc-graphic-design-130423); [Creative Boom](https://www.creativeboom.com/inspiration/uncommon-creates-triangle-based-identity-for-ea-sports-fc/)
- **FC 27 menu material:** the new **High Contrast Menus** option "uses opaque menu tiles instead of translucent ones". The default menu tiles are therefore translucent (a frosted/glass-style treatment). — [EA Accessibility – FC 27](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/ea-sports-fc/fc-27)
- **FC 27 colour accessibility:**
  - Colourblind filters (Protanopia / Deuteranopia / Tritanopia) with intensity 0–10 (default 6).
  - **High Contrast Mode**: pitch saturation from 0, which is fully greyscale, upward; a shader on "Cloth Only" or "Full Body"; user-set team, goalkeeper, ball and referee colours.
  - Brightness and contrast 0–10 (default 5).
  - Source: [EA Accessibility – FC 27](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/ea-sports-fc/fc-27)
- Press on FC 27 UI quality:
  - Insider Gaming calls the menus "god-awful" and says "the UI, interface, and menus continue to be a disjointed mess".
  - It reports text sometimes overlapping or missing.
  - It argues there is a "lack of care for the presentation because the focus is on gameplay".
  - Source: [Insider Gaming review](https://insider-gaming.com/reviews/ea-sports-fc-27-review/)
- Operation Sports calls the presentation "great" without detail, so press opinion conflicts. — [Operation Sports](https://www.operationsports.com/ea-fc-27-review-an-upgrade-not-a-revolution/)
- **Proprietary elements, do not copy:**
  - the EA SPORTS FC triangle marque and authenticity mark;
  - the bespoke F37/Uncommon typefaces, which are custom and not licensable ([F37 – EAFC](https://f37foundry.com/custom-fonts/eafc));
  - trademarked feature names such as **PlayStyles**, **HyperMotion**, **Ultimate Team** and **The Grounds**;
  - licensed league, club and competition marks and broadcast packages. EA's Premier League deal (FIFA 15 era, **prior edition**) explicitly promised "broadcast quality overlay graphics" for team sheets, formations, score and clock, next fixture and player stats. Those graphics belong to the league's broadcast identity. Source: [The Sixth Axis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2014/08/06/ea-sign-new-deal-with-the-barclays-premier-league-to-make-fifa-15-more-authentic/); [EA news – EPL partnership](https://www.ea.com/news/sports-tech-partner-eas-epl-1) (as summarised by search)

### Inferences
- Patterns that can be borrowed legally because they are generic genre conventions, not FC's trade dress:
  - dark neutral base with one green accent family;
  - a geometric marker shape reused as the brand motif;
  - a modular grid;
  - translucent panels with an opaque high-contrast fallback;
  - per-team colour customisation;
  - colour-blind-safe alternate palettes with adjustable intensity.
- For an original app, use a different marker geometry and an open-licence typeface rather than anything triangle-and-ink-trap-derived. That avoids trade-dress confusion with FC.
- The FC 27 accessibility option set (opaque tiles, greyscale pitch, custom team colours) is a ready checklist for a TV-viewed tactical display that has to read on varied screens.

### Gaps
- **No source found on in-match overlay animation**: easing, durations, slide/wipe directions, how the score bug expands into the dropdown. Gameplay footage or a designer talk would be needed.
- No hex values for the FC palette were found in the sources read. The F37 and Design Week pages were not opened.
- No source states whether the in-match HUD uses the brand typefaces, or which typeface the score bug uses.
- Whether the in-match HUD (as opposed to menus) uses gradients or a glass treatment is unknown.

## 4. Which broadcast-style graphics appear during matches (line-ups, stat pop-ups, substitutions, VAR, replays) and how do they look and move?

### Takeaway
Documented broadcast-style elements in the FC 26/27 era:
- **scrolling line-ups** at kick-off;
- the **score clock with its dropdown**;
- **tactical suggestion notifications**;
- **POV camera scenes** for bookings, set pieces, goals and saves;
- **power-shot zoom** and goal-celebration camera shake;
- a "set piece tactics interface" with a **curved delivery path** for corners.

Licensed competitions historically carry real-world broadcast overlays (team sheets, formations, score/clock, next fixture, player stats). No source fetched this session describes how FC 27 substitution, VAR or replay graphics look or animate.

### Cited Findings
- **Scrolling Line Ups**: a toggle that shows line-ups scrolling at match start (FC 26, prior edition). — [FIFPlay (community)](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-26-game-settings/)
- **POV Camera Scenes**: "first-person scenes for bookings, set pieces, goals, and saves", which can be turned off. **Camera Shake** happens during goal celebrations. **Power Shot Zoom** is a dynamic camera move on Power Shots. All are FC 27. — [EA Accessibility – FC 27](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/ea-sports-fc/fc-27)
- Corner kicks in FC 27 show a "set piece tactics interface" and a "curved delivery path and kick controls". The camera "will shift towards the box" after a delivery area is chosen, then "returns to your preferred gameplay camera" after a short corner. — [EA Pitch Notes: Gameplay Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FC 27 camera styles include Competitive, **Broadcast**, Pro and Tactical, each changing field of view and camera dynamism. Custom Height and Zoom settings exist. — [EA Accessibility – FC 27](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/ea-sports-fc/fc-27)
- An official FC 27 marketing image caption reads: "Broadcast cameras and a packed stadium capture the post-match moment under bright floodlights." This is marketing art, not a feature description. — [EA SPORTS FC 27 official page](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27)
- **Prior edition (FC 26):** a PS5 review says competition broadcast presentation is "clean", "especially the Champions League with its new format". — [Football Fashion review (FC 26)](https://footballfashion.org/wordpress/2025/09/28/review-ea-sports-fc-26-ps5/) (as summarised by search)
- **Prior edition (FIFA 15):** the Premier League deal promised overlays "synonymous with international TV broadcasts": team sheets, formations, score and clock, next fixture, and player stats. — [The Sixth Axis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2014/08/06/ea-sign-new-deal-with-the-barclays-premier-league-to-make-fifa-15-more-authentic/)

### Inferences
- The FC broadcast layer runs in three tiers:
  1. persistent: score clock;
  2. event-driven transient: dropdown, notifications, line-ups at kick-off;
  3. camera/cinematic: POV scenes, replays, celebration camera.

  That is a useful model for Balomer's live radar plus timed alerts plus halftime/full-time reports.
- Licensed competitions get their real broadcaster or league overlay look in-game, so the visual style of the score bug varies by competition. Treat any competition-specific look as proprietary.

### Gaps
- **Substitution, VAR, injury, card, stat pop-up and replay graphics for FC 27 (or FC 26) were not found** in any fetched source: no description of layout, colours or animation.
