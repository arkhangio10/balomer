# Set pieces in EA SPORTS FC 27 (and FIFA 23 to FC 26) and free-kick graphics in live TV broadcasts (as of 21 Oct 2026)

Scope note: "FC 27" means EA SPORTS FC 27, released September 2026. FC 26, FC 25, FC 24 and FIFA 23 are labelled as **prior edition** wherever they appear. The current set-piece system dates from the **FIFA 23** rework, and EA has published little about set pieces since. The best current primary source is EA's own help article on free kicks, which is undated beyond "Updated 1 month ago" when read on 21 Oct 2026. The other primary sources are the FC 26 and FC 27 Pitch Notes deep dives. Most per-element detail (labels, stick mappings, the penalty ring) comes from third-party guides written for FIFA 23 and FC 24/25. Fetched text sources say almost nothing about the on-screen shapes, colours or motion; that is recorded as a gap. "(snippet)" marks a claim that comes only from a search-result summary because the page itself was not fetched or could not be read.

## 1. In FC 27 (and FC 26/FC 25), what UI appears when taking a free kick, a corner and a penalty, and is a trajectory or curve preview shown?

### Takeaway
**Free kicks:** the current game (FC 27, per EA's live help article) shows an **on-screen trajectory line** as the main aiming aid. The player sets aim, a **contact point on the ball** and **power**. Since FIFA 23 the preview is deliberately **short**: it covers only the first few metres of flight. A **Dead Ball** PlayStyle lengthens it, and Dead Ball+ takes it to maximum length. Guides for prior editions also describe a **text label that names the shot type** and a **panel with the taker's stats**.

**Corners in FC 27** keep the same aim, spin and power line. What is new is that after the target is picked, the **camera moves toward the box** and gives a short window to control attackers, including a new **Avoidance** input.

**Penalties** use a **pulsing "composure ring"** around the ball instead of a reticle. This is documented for FIFA 23 and FC 24; no source confirms whether it changed in FC 25–27.

### Cited Findings
**Free kicks: current (EA help article, applies to the live game)**
- EA's numbered steps are: choose the taker; "Aim your free kick. Move your aim toward the part of the goal you want to target"; "Choose how you want to strike the ball. Adjusting the contact point changes the spin and trajectory of your free kick"; "Set the power. Release when you've added the amount of power you want"; "Watch the trajectory and adjust your next attempt." — [EA Help: How to take free kicks in EA SPORTS FC](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)
- "Use the on-screen trajectory line as a guide when setting up your free kick. Experiment with different contact points to learn how they affect the ball's curve and height. The best combination depends on your distance and angle from the goal, as well as the player taking the free kick." — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)
- "A player with the Dead Ball PlayStyle can also perform set pieces with increased speed, curve, and accuracy. It also gives you a longer trajectory preview when taking a set piece." — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)
- FUTBIN's FC 27 PlayStyle page: base Dead Ball gives "Ball trajectory preview line is longer", and PlayStyle+ gives "Ball trajectory preview line is at maximum length". The FC 26 page uses the same wording. (snippet) — [FUTBIN FC 27 Dead Ball](https://www.futbin.com/27/playstyles/dead-ball); [FUTBIN FC 26 Dead Ball](https://www.futbin.com/26/playstyles/dead-ball)
- A **quick free kick** can be taken "instead of waiting for the full setup". — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)

**Free kicks: the UI as introduced in FIFA 23 (prior edition)**
- "The circular reticle is … being scrapped in favor of a more intuitive trajectory line", which the left stick adjusts. The right stick picks the strike point and spin. "A text box names the shot type", from a curler to a "ferocious knuckleball", and "a new panel shows the taker's stats". — [Charlie INTEL: FIFA 23 set pieces](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/)
- An aiming reticle is "placed slightly behind the ball", and its position shows where the boot will strike the ball. **Corners: FC 27 (current)**
- "Corners in FC 27 are designed to give you more control over the moments before the ball arrives in the box." After choosing the delivery target, "the camera will shift towards the box, giving you a brief window to control attacking players". In that window you can move, switch player, make runs, shield, or use Avoidance. The camera shift does not happen on short corners. — [EA Pitch Notes: FC 27 Gameplay Deep Dive (29 Jul 2026)](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive)
- **Avoidance** is new: it makes the attacker or defender you control more evasive, so they move through crowds without being blocked. During corners it is held on R1/RB. Players in the box "no longer automatically move into the best position to connect with the ball". — [EA Pitch Notes FC 27](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive)
- "Crosses will now be delivered into space more often rather than directly onto a specific player". Aim and power have more effect on where a cross lands, and headers from corners "require greater precision". — [EA Pitch Notes FC 27](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive)

**Corners: base system from FIFA 23 (prior edition)**
- The left stick moves the trajectory line horizontally to pick a target. The right stick adds spin (for example a float to the back post, or an outswinger). "Power applied also determines roughly where the corner will land". A text box describes the cross type. — [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/)

**Penalties (FIFA 23 and FC 24, prior editions; nothing found for FC 25–27)**
- Penalties use an "expanding and shrinking composure ring" around the ball. Pressing Shoot when the ring is smallest gives maximum accuracy; mistiming sends the ball high. The ring's speed depends on the taker's skill and on how important the kick is. The left stick aims; R1/RB or L1/LB with Shoot gives a finesse or chip shot. — [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/)
- FC 24 guides describe the same thing: a composure ring at the penalty spot, shoot when it is smallest, and no aiming reticle. A higher Penalties attribute forgives a larger ring. (snippet) — [WePC: FC 24 penalties](https://www.wepc.com/gaming/ea-sports-fc-24-penalties/); [Common Sense Gamer](https://commonsensegamer.com/mastering-the-dark-arts-of-penalties-in-fc-24/)
- EA's FC 26 deep dive does not mention penalties. — [EA Pitch Notes: FC 26 Gameplay Deep Dive (22 Jul 2025)](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive). EA's FC 27 deep dive covers corners only and does not mention penalties. — [EA Pitch Notes FC 27](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive)

**Practice tools**
- **FC 27 (new):** a dedicated **Free Kick Practice** session, labelled "New in FC 27", for practising "from different positions around the pitch". — [FIFPlay: FC 27 Practice Arena](https://www.fifplay.com/fc-27-practice-arena/)
- **FC 26 (prior):** the kick taker can be changed on the fly in the Practice Arena, and corners can now be taken there. — [EA Pitch Notes FC 26](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)

### Inferences
- The UI grammar has been stable since FIFA 23. It has four parts: (a) a direction line on the pitch, (b) a marker for where the boot meets the ball, (c) a power meter that charges while the button is held, and (d) a short text label naming the technique. Corners and free kicks share the grammar; penalties use a separate timing ring.
- The visual grammar is "short preview, long consequence": the line shows the start of the flight and the player has to predict the rest. That is a deliberate skill gate, and Dead Ball / Dead Ball+ act as a stat-driven extension of the line.

- **Shot-type labels:** the full current list is unconfirmed. Confirmed labels are "curler", "knuckleball" and "Inside Foot Curled Shot" (all FIFA 23). - **Penalties in FC 25/26/27:** no official source says whether the composure ring was changed, kept or removed.
- **Free Kick Practice (FC 27):** what it shows on screen (distance, statistics, replays) is not documented in the fetched source.

## 2. How do aim, power and curve/spin work (input → displayed feedback → ball flight), which edition introduced the system, and what has changed since?

### Takeaway
There are three inputs: **left stick = direction**, **right stick = contact point on the ball**, and **hold-and-release shoot = power**. The contact point decides the shot type. Hitting the ball low and to the side curls it up and around the wall; hitting it near the top keeps it flat and low. Each input has its own feedback: the trajectory line, the contact-point marker with its shot-type label, and the power meter (guides count it in "bars").

The system came in with **FIFA 23**, replacing the circular-reticle aiming used up to FIFA 22. Later changes:
- **FC 24:** added the Dead Ball PlayStyle, which lengthens the preview.
- **FC 26:** removed **Timed Finishing**, which was the second press that set-piece guides had used, and adjusted free-kick trajectories to compensate.
- **FC 27:** reworked corners (camera window plus Avoidance) and added Free Kick Practice. A "precision upgrade" for free kicks is claimed only by one secondary source.

### Cited Findings
**Input mapping (FIFA 23 onward)**
- The right stick "controls the player's foot placement on the ball. This changes the height, curve, and angle the ball will travel." (snippet, PlayStation Competition Center guide; the URL now redirects) — [PlayStation Compete FIFA 23 article](https://compete.playstation.com/en-us/fifa_23/articles/228787)
- For a right-footed taker, striking the bottom right of the ball gives an "Inside Foot Curled Shot" that rises and curls left. Striking the top of the ball gives a flat trajectory that stays low, to go under the wall. — [FUT.GG](https://fut.gg/news/fifa-23-guide-how-to-take-free-kicks)
- Aiming the right stick to the right curls the shot over the wall; aiming near the top sneaks it under. Hold Shoot to build power, and in FIFA 23 press Shoot again for Timed Finishing. — [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/)
- EA's FIFA 23 feature page lets you "choose where you make contact on the ball to change direction, trajectory, curl, and more". (snippet; the page now redirects to an FC 27 hub, so the wording was not checked) — [EA FIFA 23 gameplay features](https://www.ea.com/games/fifa/fifa-23/new-features-and-modes/gameplay-features)

**Attributes that turn input into flight (FIFA 23 guide)**
- "Curve" sets the maximum curve and the side spin or backspin. "Shot Power" sets ball speed. "Crossing" and "Free Kick Accuracy" affect the maximum elevation, which keeps higher-power kicks under control. — [FUT.GG](https://fut.gg/news/fifa-23-guide-how-to-take-free-kicks)
- The current EA help article names Free Kick Accuracy, Curve and Shot Power as the attributes that matter. — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)

**Power in "bars" (FC 25 and FC 26 community guides, prior editions)**
- The FC 25 guides disagree:
  - Red Bull quotes RedLac: about 1.5 bars on the edge of the box and 2.5 bars from further out. Aim at the last man in the wall with the left stick, then push the right stick toward a bottom corner of the "grid". (snippet) — [Red Bull: FC 25 set piece guide](https://www.redbull.com/gb-en/ea-sports-fc-25-set-piece-guide)
  - Team Gullit says about 3 bars, and to press shoot again just before contact to hit the "green zone" for timing. (snippet) — [Team Gullit: FC 25 set pieces](https://www.teamgullit.com/ea-sports-fc-25/set-pieces)
- FC 26 guides: 3 bars at short range and 3.5 bars further out. (snippet; low-reliability site) — [Vocal Media FC 26 free kick guide](https://vocal.media/gamers/fc-26-a-guide-to-free-kicks-in-ea-fc-26)

**Changes by edition**
- **Up to FIFA 22:** aiming used a circular reticle. FIFA 23 "scrapped" it for a trajectory line. — [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/)
- **FIFA 23:** the contact-point system above, a shot-type text box, the taker stats panel, a player lying down behind the wall, and goals scored directly from corners became possible. — [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/features/fifa-23-puts-the-threat-back-into-free-kicks-and-corners) (snippet only; the fetched page body was truncated, so the corner-goal claim is unverified)
- **FC 24:** PlayStyles were introduced. EA's FC 24 PlayStyles deep dive says "all effects listed are for both PlayStyle and PlayStyle+ versions, unless specified, with the PlayStyle+ versions being increased". Per the search summary it also describes Dead Ball as giving a longer preview line on free kicks and corners. (snippet; the fetched copy did not include the Dead Ball paragraph) — [EA: FC 24 PlayStyles deep dive](https://www.ea.com/news/fc-24-playstyles-deepdive)
- **FC 26:**
  - "Timed Finishing has been removed." The reason given: it "didn't add meaningful skill depth and proved difficult to balance" and "disproportionately rewarded long-range shots".
  - Free-kick trajectories were adjusted "after the removal of Timed Finishing".
  - "Low Driven shots will be performed by double-tapping the shot button", now "a global* shot modifier" (*except Chip Shots).
  - The Competitive preset makes corner outcomes more variable, "with fewer attacking players involved by default".
  - FC IQ corner assignments were added: Top of Box and Defensive Cover (attacking); Threat Marker, Post Watcher, Near Post and Back Post (defending).
  - All points: [EA Pitch Notes FC 26 (22 Jul 2025)](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-26/news/pitch-notes-fc26-gameplay-deep-dive)
- **FC 26 caveat:** a third-party guide notes that EA's notes do not say whether penalties follow the Timed Finishing and Low Driven changes. — [FIFPlay: FC 26 penalty shootout guide](https://www.fifplay.com/article-fc-26-penalty-shootout-guide/)
- **FC 27:** the corner camera window, Avoidance (R1/RB), no auto-positioning in the box, crosses delivered into space, and stricter headers. — [EA Pitch Notes FC 27](https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive)
- **FC 27 (single secondary source):** "new precision upgrades for headers and free kicks" that "should drastically ramp up your chances of getting a ball on target". The source credits EA but cites no EA document. EA's own deep dive, as fetched, does not mention free kicks. Treat as **unconfirmed**. — [Red Bull: EA FC 27 most exciting improvements (14 Aug 2026)](https://www.redbull.com/au-en/ea-fc-27-most-exciting-improvements)
- **Assignments:** FC 27 lets you pre-assign set-piece takers (penalties, free kicks, corners). (snippet) — [FIFAUTeam: FC 27 Assignments](https://fifauteam.com/fc-27-assignments/)

### Inferences
- The core design decision is to separate **where the ball starts** (line), **how it is struck** (contact point, which sets the spin type) and **how hard** (power). Each input has its own feedback widget, so the player can always see which knob produced which result. That is a useful pattern for explaining a free kick after the fact: show direction, contact or technique, and speed as three separate readouts.
- After FC 26 removed Timed Finishing, the **"green zone" timing in the FC 25 guides probably no longer applies** to free kicks in FC 26/27. This is inferred from EA's FC 26 note that free-kick trajectories were adjusted after the removal; no source states it for set pieces explicitly.

### Gaps
- No official EA document gives the mapping from contact point to the full list of shot types, or how many power "bars" the meter has. The "bars" convention comes only from community guides.
- No source found states whether the trajectory preview line itself (its default length or curvature) changed between FIFA 23 and FC 27, beyond Dead Ball lengthening it.
- The FC 27 "precision upgrades for free kicks" claim could not be confirmed against an EA source.

## 3. Does the game show any visible "calculation" (distance, angle, predicted path, probability) in matches, skill games, the practice arena or replays?

### Takeaway
The only predicted path shown is the **truncated trajectory preview**: it covers the first few metres and is longer with Dead Ball or Dead Ball+. Beyond that, the player sees **labels and stats, not numbers about the situation**: the shot-type name, the taker's attributes, and the power bars. I found **no source** saying FC 27 or earlier editions show distance to goal, wall distance, angle, ball speed or scoring probability on the set-piece screen, in practice, or in replays.

### Cited Findings
- The preview line shows "how the ball will travel" over the first few metres only. — [FUT.GG](https://fut.gg/news/fifa-23-guide-how-to-take-free-kicks); [Charlie INTEL](https://www.charlieintel.com/fifa-23/fifa-23-set-pieces-free-kicks-corners-penalties-188966/)
- The preview gets longer with the Dead Ball PlayStyle, and reaches maximum length with PlayStyle+. — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/); [FUTBIN FC 27](https://www.futbin.com/27/playstyles/dead-ball) (snippet)
- EA's official learning loop is trial and error: "Try taking several free kicks from the same position. Change one part of your technique at a time … This can help you see which adjustment changes the ball's trajectory". No numeric feedback is mentioned. — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)
- EA's help text also says the right combination "depends on your distance and angle from the goal". Distance and angle are framed as things the player judges, not values the game displays. — [EA Help](https://help.ea.com/en/articles/ea-sports-fc/free-kicks/)

### Inferences
- The game hides the physics on purpose so that reading the curve is the skill. An analytics product has the opposite goal and should **show** distance, angle and speed. The game's grammar can still be borrowed: a short, honest "what we know" line plus a named technique label, with no false precision.

### Gaps
- I found no source on whether FC 27's Free Kick Practice or the Practice Arena shows a landing marker, a speed readout or a success rate.
- I found no source on replay overlays for set pieces (for example ball-path tracers) in FC 24–27.
- Skill Games, the older mini-games with numeric scoring targets: I did not check their status or set-piece content in FC 24–27.

## 4. How do real broadcasts show free-kick distance, wall distance, ball speed, trajectory and set-piece probability?

### Takeaway
Broadcast free-kick graphics come from **camera-calibrated AR (augmented reality) tools** that draw graphics on the pitch: a **free-kick circle** (the 9.15 m exclusion radius), a **distance-to-goal** line or arc, an overhead "goal arc", and **speed & distance** callouts. The main sources here are Ross Video PIERO and Vizrt Viz Arena; Second Spectrum, TrackMan and Hawk-Eye appear below for tracking and speed. They tie the graphics to the pitch by calibrating against pitch lines.

Live **numbers** come from tracking providers. Examples: Second Spectrum in the Premier League, and the DFL with AWS for the "Bundesliga Match Facts". Shot Speed is measured at peak, right after the strike, and shots over 100 km/h are highlighted. "Set Piece Threat" rates a team's free-kick and corner danger against the league average and is shown **before** free kicks and corners. I found no confirmed live "free-kick xG" or wall-distance-in-metres graphic from a named broadcaster.

### Cited Findings
**Rule baseline**
- Under the Laws of the Game, opponents must stay at least **9.15 m (10 yd)** from the ball until it is in play, unless they are on their own goal line between the posts. (snippet) — [The FA: Law 13 Free Kicks (2026-27)](https://www.thefa.com/-/media/files/thefaportal/governance-docs/laws-of-the-game/2026-27/law-13---free-kicks.ashx)
- The Premier League adopted **vanishing spray** so referees can mark the free-kick spot and the 9.15 m wall line. It is a physical marking, not a graphic. (snippet) — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/sport/football/english-premier-league-adopts-vanishing-spray-1.1365775)

**AR graphics vendors**
- **Ross Video PIERO:**
  - The Club brochure lists "goal arc overhead", "free kick circle" and "speed & distance" among its effects, along with offside line, 3D overhead, arrows and time lapse. — [PIERO Club brochure (PDF)](https://documentation.rossvideo.com/files/Brochures/Graphics%20Systems/PIERO/Piero_Club_Brochure.pdf) (snippet)
  - The Live brochure says graphics for "offside lines, laser offside walls, free kick circles, and distance to goals" can go to air in seconds. — [PIERO Live brochure (PDF)](https://documentation.rossvideo.com/files/Brochures/Graphics%20Systems/PIERO/Piero_Live_Brochure.pdf) (snippet)
  - The Broadcast brochure says it uses a proprietary line-and-texture tracking algorithm co-developed with BBC R&D, and graphics "stay tied to the game as the camera moves". — [PIERO Broadcast brochure (PDF)](https://documentation.rossvideo.com/files/Brochures/Graphics%20Systems/PIERO/Piero_Broadcast_Brochure.pdf) (snippet)
  - It works with data from OPTA, TracAB, Second Spectrum, STATS and StatCast. (snippet) — same Broadcast brochure
- **Vizrt Viz Arena:** the product listing includes "Offside lines & freekick distance" among its AR graphics. It calibrates broadcast cameras against the pitch and keys graphics in without camera sensors. The product page returned 404 when fetched. **Tracking data feeding live numbers**
- **Second Spectrum** is the Premier League's official tracking provider. It captures player and ball positions **25 times a second** using computer vision. Broadcasters get augmentation that includes "live statistics such as player speeds and shot velocities". (snippet) — [Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/news/premier-league-tv-coverage-set-for-real-time-stats-revolution-thanks-to-skeletal-tracking-tech-4277923); [SVG Europe](https://www.svgeurope.org/blog/headlines/premier-league-launches-new-metrics-and-insights-for-fans-with-stats-perform-and-second-spectrum/)

  - Exceptionally high speeds are highlighted right after the shot. The Data Story Finder flags goals "above 100 kilometres per hour (62 miles per hour)".
  - It also credits goalkeepers who save fast shots, and keeps matchday and season rankings.
  - Source: [SVG: DFL & AWS add Shot Speed](https://www.sportsvideo.org/2023/11/03/dfl-aws-add-new-shot-speed-stat-to-bundesliga-match-facts/)

  - It can be shown live, for example **before free kicks and corners**, to show how dangerous the situation is.
  - The formula and the visual format are not described.
  - Sources: [Bundesliga.com: DFL and AWS add Set Piece Threat and Skill](https://www.bundesliga.com/en/bundesliga/news/dfl-and-aws-add-set-piece-threat-and-skill-match-facts-19067); [Broadcast Now](https://broadcastnow.co.uk/production/bundesliga-adds-set-piece-threat-and-skill-match-facts/5168243.article) (snippet)
- **Hawk-Eye** in the Premier League: the only broadcast graphic I found documented is the goal-line technology graphic, a replay shown within about 20 s of the ball crossing the line. I found no free-kick graphic from Hawk-Eye. (snippet) — [Graphic News](https://www.graphicnews.com/en/pages/21254/soccer-hawk-eye-watches-premier-league); [WIPO Magazine](https://www.wipo.int/web/wipo-magazine/articles/goal-line-technology-getting-it-right-37357)

**Research and technique relevant to an original implementation**
- A live-broadcast directing paper draws the ball's travel trajectory on the current frame "based on the collective ball positions of the current and past frames". It estimates real-world shooting distance by calibrating the camera against field markings. (snippet) — [Smart Director, arXiv 2201.04024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.04024)
- A study of Bundesliga free kicks (2013/14–2014/15, n = 1624) found **no statistical advantage for the defence** when the wall stood closer than 9.15 m, or when the rule was broken. (snippet) — [BISp SURF: A topography of free kicks in soccer](https://www.bisp-surf.de/Record/PU201612008805)
- Queen's University Belfast research found that a free kick is harder to save when the wall blocks the goalkeeper's first view of the ball. (snippet) — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/football-the-wall-can-make-it-harder-to-save-free-kicks-new-research-151869); [QUB news](https://www.qub.ac.uk/about/Leadership-and-structure/Faculties-and-Schools/Engineering-and-Physical-Sciences/News/Archive/Queensresearchanswersthegoalkeeperswallconundruminfreekicks.html)
- A European patent describes a device that projects a **9.15 m circle** around the ball onto the pitch. — [EP 3180096 B1: Free kick distance projecting device](https://data.epo.org/gpi/EP3180096B1) (snippet)

### Inferences
- **Patterns Balomer can borrow** (Balomer's input is a fixed camera, a pitch homography and 2D coordinates):
  - (1) A **9.15 m free-kick circle** drawn on the radar around the ball's spot.
  - (2) A **distance-to-goal** label (metres from the spot to the centre of the goal) plus the **goal-mouth angle**, the angle the goal posts subtend from the spot.
  - (3) **Measured wall distance**: from the ball to the nearest line of defenders, compared against 9.15 m.
  - (4) A **post-hoc shot vector or tracer** from tracked ball positions, drawn from past frames as in the Smart Director paper rather than predicted.
  - (5) A Set-Piece-Threat-style **team rate versus a baseline**, shown before the kick. These are all deterministic numbers, which fits the CLAUDE.md rule that the cloud agent writes prose but never produces a number.
- **Physical limits of a single fixed camera with a ground-plane homography:** it cannot measure ball height, so a 3D arc over the wall cannot be reconstructed honestly. Any "speed" figure is the speed of the ball's projection on the ground, and it is limited by frame rate and by how often the ball is detected (a fast strike crosses many metres between frames). For comparison, broadcasters use 25 Hz optical tracking or radar. Label a speed figure as "approximate horizontal speed", or leave it out.
- **Do not copy (IP flags):**
  - EA's assets: the EA SPORTS FC / FIFA names and marks, the PlayStyle / PlayStyle+ names and icons ("Dead Ball" as EA's branded PlayStyle and its icon), EA's HUD art, fonts and colours, and the specific look of the composure ring.
  - League and broadcaster branding: Premier League, Bundesliga, the "Bundesliga Match Facts powered by AWS" lock-up, Sky Sports, NBC.
  - Vendor product names and their graphic packages: PIERO, Viz Arena, Virtual 1st, Second Spectrum, TrackMan, Hawk-Eye.
  - Naming metrics: "Set Piece Threat" and "Shot Speed" are DFL/AWS product names. Use generic descriptive names, for example "set-piece danger vs. baseline" or "strike speed".
  - The 9.15 m circle, distance lines and angle arcs are generic geometry taken from the Laws of the Game. Note that a patent exists on a *physical* 9.15 m projection device (EP 3180096). This is not legal advice.

### Gaps
- **Free-kick xG or probability on air:** I found no confirmed live free-kick xG graphic, or "probability of scoring this free kick" graphic, from a named broadcaster.
- **Wall distance in metres on air:** I found no confirmed broadcast graphic that shows the measured ball-to-wall distance as a number. Vendors list "free kick circle" and "distance to goals", but those are snippets from brochures, not screen captures.
- **Visual conventions:** the colours, line styles and typography of the PIERO and Viz free-kick circles and goal arcs are not documented in the sources reached.
- **Trajectory tracers in football:** no source documents a live free-kick ball-flight tracer like golf's or the NFL kicking tracer in Premier League, LaLiga or UEFA coverage. The only documented examples are the NFL tracer (TrackMan) and academic prototypes.
- **Sky Sports, ESPN, LaLiga, UEFA:** I did not reach any primary source describing their specific free-kick graphics within the research budget.
